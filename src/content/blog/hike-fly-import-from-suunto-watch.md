Sharing **Hike&Fly activities** recorded with your **Suunto watch** is easier now — thanks to the new **Suunto import**.

## Follow these steps to set up Suunto import

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Connect with Suunto**.
3. Connect your **Suunto account**. An in-app browser opens so you can enter your Suunto credentials and allow **Sidekik** access to your activities.
4. From then on you get a **notification in the notification centre** whenever a **hike activity** from your watch is uploaded to your Suunto account.

**Important:** Only activities whose **Suunto type is “Hiking”, “Running”, “Trail running”, “Ski touring” or “Walking”** can be imported at the moment.

## Import a new activity

1. When a new Suunto activity is ready to import, you get a **notification** — both as a **push notification** and in the **notification centre**.
2. To bring it into **Sidekik**, tap **“Import”**.
3. After the import the activity appears in the **feed** and is visible to you and others. Under the map, the **Suunto logo** shows that it came from **Suunto**.
