“Sidekick” is another word for “wingman”. Sidekik is there to support, motivate and guide you on your paragliding adventures. That vision is a step closer today — with the beta launch of **Hike&Fly tracking**, right in the app.

## Support in the air

Tracking starts with a set of functions that help your flying and your Peak Hunt challenges.

### Challenge peaks on the map

Challenge waypoints (peaks) are visible on the map — including the radius and automatic zoom when you get close. You see the current distance and the closest you have come to that peak on this flight.

### Obstacles on the map

Safety first: the new map shows potential obstacles such as power lines and cable cars. You can plan a better line and, in the air, see what is ahead. (Data: https://airspace.xcontest.org/)

![Obstacle display with power lines and cable cars on the Sidekik tracking map](../../assets/blog/hindernisse.png)

### See where it (probably) goes up

With KK7 thermal maps you see at a glance where lift is most likely — based on real user data from more than three million XC flights (data: https://thermal.kk7.ch/).

### Airspace

To help you stay clear of airspace, you can sync current airspace and show it on the map. (Data: https://www.openaip.net/)

### XC distance

Other iOS flying apps often miss this, and it always motivated me in the air (when I used XCTrack), so it is there from day one. Every 30 seconds the current XC distance is calculated and shown (including whether it is a free distance, a flat triangle or an FAI triangle). To save battery it can differ slightly from the XContest calculation.

## Also

**Privacy**

You only need to give Sidekik location access **while using the app**. We do nothing in the background — if the app is closed, it is closed.

**Offline mode**

If you have no internet when you finish the track, the flight is stored on the device. Next time you connect, it syncs automatically.
