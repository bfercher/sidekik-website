If you fly, you collect data. If you want to get better, you look closer.

That is why the last weeks were spent on one topic: statistics.

The result: a clearer overview, more ways to compare and more depth — for you, your club and your challenges.

## Personal statistics: rebuilt

Personal stats were redesigned from scratch. Instead of scattered numbers you get a clearer structure and more useful charts.

- A better overview of your flights and activities
- A clearer split by flight type (Hike & Fly, XC and so on)
- Your progress in one chart, with many filters

In short: less searching, more understanding.

You will find the new statistics in your personal profile (bottom right, tap the profile picture) under “Statistics” → “Show all statistics”.

## Club leaderboards: more filters, more comparison

Club statistics changed a lot too.

You can now see what is happening in the club with more precision:

- extra filters by period, flight type and more
- better comparison between members
- more transparency about how active the club really is

Ideal if you like looking at numbers together — or a little in-club comparison.

You will find club leaderboards on the club profile → “Top 3” section → “Show all rankings”.

## Challenge statistics: extra stats and leaderboards

The biggest change is challenge statistics.

There is now a dedicated, rebuilt stats page with:

- extra rankings
- a clearer view of the results
- a better overview of performance across the whole challenge

Whether you are at the front or just tracking your own progress — the new challenge stats show more than places.

You will find them on the challenge screen, below the map: “Show challenge statistics >”.

## Why we built this

Sidekik should not only collect flights. It should motivate. Good statistics make goals tangible and progress visible.

Have fun clicking through, comparing and discovering 🚀
