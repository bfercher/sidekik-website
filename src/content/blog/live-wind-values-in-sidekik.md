Wind is one of the decisive factors in paragliding and Hike & Fly. It shapes launch decisions, route choice, how you use thermals — and safety. With the latest update, Sidekik shows live wind values on the map, so you always have the conditions around you.

## Wind information where you need it

Instead of switching apps or websites, live wind is now in Sidekik tracking. Arrows show direction, coloured circles show strength. At a glance you get a feel for launch sites, your route and possible landing zones.

## Built for a quick read

On the mountain, overview matters. Sidekik uses a clear visual: direction arrows plus a colour scale so you can spot stronger wind, sheltered areas or sharp differences — without a long analysis.

The edge of the circle is the maximum gust, the fill is the wind. Below that you see the numbers: left of the / is the wind, right are the gusts in km/h.

### Colour coding

- Blue: wind up to 6 km/h, gusts up to 14 km/h
- Green: wind up to 14 km/h, gusts up to 24 km/h
- Yellow: wind up to 24 km/h, gusts up to 32 km/h
- Orange: wind up to 30 km/h, gusts up to 38 km/h
- Red: wind up to 36 km/h, gusts up to 44 km/h
- Violet: everything above

## More safety when you plan and when you fly

Live wind helps in several phases:

- Before launch: whether the conditions match your skill and your plan.
- In flight: a better read of the air.
- On Hike & Fly: wind exposure on ridges, summits and crossings before you launch.

## Coverage today

Live wind readings are currently available in Austria. The data comes from official GeoSphere stations and updates every 10 minutes. More regions — Germany, Switzerland and extra stations in Austria — are already in progress.

## Another step toward situational awareness

Live wind does not replace a forecast or your own judgement on site. It adds to your picture exactly where it helps most: on the map, in the context of your flight. Another building block for planning, orientation and safety.

👉 In short: fewer app switches, more overview — and better decisions on the mountain.
