Sharing **Hike&Fly activities** recorded with your **Garmin watch** is easier now — thanks to the new **Garmin Connect import**.

## Follow these steps to set up Garmin Connect import

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Garmin Connect [Beta]**.
3. Connect your **Garmin Connect account**. An in-app browser opens so you can enter your Garmin Connect credentials and allow **Sidekik** access to your activities.
4. From then on you get a **notification in the notification centre** whenever you upload a **hike activity** to Garmin Connect.

**Important:** Only activities whose **Garmin type was “Hiking”** can be imported. **Changing the type later** in Garmin has **no effect** on the import.

## Import a new activity

1. When a new Garmin activity is ready to import, you get a **notification** — both as a **push notification** and in the **notification centre**.
2. You can preview the activity. To bring it into **Sidekik**, tap **“Import”**. If it is a hike you do **not** want in Sidekik, choose **“Ignore”**.
3. After the import the activity appears in the **feed** and is visible to you and others. Under the map, the **Garmin logo** shows that it came from **Garmin**.
